Privacy Policy

RT Inventory

Last updated: October 22, 2026

RT Inventory is an internal application of RT Technologies LTD, Grunthal, Manitoba, Canada. It is used only by RT Technologies staff and connects only to RT Technologies' own QuickBooks Online company.

What data it accesses. With the company administrator's approval (Intuit sign-in), RT Inventory reads RT Technologies' QuickBooks Online accounting data: items, vendors, customers, accounts, tax codes, classes, currencies, purchase orders, bills, invoices and journal entries. This includes customer and vendor business contact details already held in QuickBooks.

Why. To keep RT Technologies' inventory and cost records in step with its accounting, and to reconcile the two. The data is not used for marketing, profiling, advertising or any purpose outside RT Technologies' own operations.

Where it is stored. On RT Technologies' own server in Canada, in a database that only RT Technologies' systems and staff can reach. The QuickBooks access tokens are encrypted on that server (Windows Data Protection) and are never written into program code or logs.

Sharing. The data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone outside RT Technologies, except service providers RT Technologies already uses to run its business (for example Intuit itself) or where the law requires it.

Retention. Data is kept as long as RT Technologies needs it for its records and legal obligations. Disconnecting RT Inventory from QuickBooks stops all further access; data already copied stays in RT Technologies' records under the same protections.

Security. Access is limited to RT Technologies staff. The QuickBooks connection uses Intuit's OAuth 2.0 sign-in; RT Inventory never sees or stores a QuickBooks password.
